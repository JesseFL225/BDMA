# Suggested Section 5 paragraph

Regularised multinomial logistic regression predicted 102 fixed duration classes using 33,004 training events from 2011-2023. Training-only chronological validation on 2022 and 2023 selected ridge alpha=0 and lambda=0.001 for both feature sets. On the common 2024 validation cohort (n=5,296), time-only regression achieved log loss 3.2173 and accuracy 11.78%, versus 3.2309 and 11.73% for the global baseline. Archived-context regression achieved log loss 3.0820, accuracy 12.76% and macro-F1 0.0096; its log loss was slightly worse than the cause-group baseline (3.0716), despite higher accuracy. Thus, the time-only model provided a small probability-quality improvement, whereas the archived-context model did not improve on its relevant baseline under the primary metric. Rare-class stabilisation added one pseudo-count per class at the training-feature mean. Archived information may have been revised after onset, so retrospective results do not establish real-time deployability. The 2025 outcomes remained unexamined.
